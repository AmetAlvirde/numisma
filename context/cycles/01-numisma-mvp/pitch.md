# Numisma MVP Pitch

Problem: Multi-asset traders manage capital, risk, positions, and decisions
across scattered tools that do not produce one reliable operating record.

Who: An individual crypto-native trader/investor operating systematic and
discretionary strategies across multiple time horizons, platforms, and risk
regimes.

Gap: Existing trackers, dashboards, spreadsheets, journals, and charting tools
show fragments of portfolio state but do not connect capital ownership,
portfolio mandates, tempo-specific execution, position lineage, and decision
history.

Distinction: Numisma treats trading as Fund-level capital discipline across
Portfolios, Tempos, Strategies, Positions, Lots, Perspectives, Closes, and audit
history rather than as passive balance tracking.

Form / access surface: The first access surface is a local-first TUI with manual
entry for Positions, Closes, prices, balances, and journal context.

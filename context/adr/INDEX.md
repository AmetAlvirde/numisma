| [ADR-003](./ADR-003-event-log-genesis-fold-persistence.md) | Append-only event log, immutable genesis seed, and fold to the review model | product | Accepted (amended 2026-07-01, 2026-07-02, 2026-07-02) | Portfolio history is an append-only event log on an immutable genesis seed; current and as-of state are a pure fold into the existing `FundReviewData`. | [ADR-004](./ADR-004-preferences-sidecar.md) | Preferences sidecar: trader policy as a durable artifact beside the event log | product | Accepted | Trader policy (profit-split ratio, loss-netting `splitBasis`) is persisted in a second durable artifact `data/preferences.jsonl` beside the event log, with its own append-only + on-load-validation contract and a pure `pickPolicyAsOf(prefs, asOf)` as-of selector. Policy lives OUTSIDE the immutable log so the fold stays pure and the log folds standalone to the pure #90 book; the profit-split obligation is a compose-time, descriptive-only derivation, never re-added to NAV. Supersedes the grill's genesis-field idea and the deferred `ProfitPolicySet` 8th verb. Trade-offs: log-purity + no 8th verb vs a folded verb; `effectiveAt` time-versioning vs an immutable genesis field. ADR-001 split: `pickPolicyAsOf`/`composeProfitSplit` pure in `@numisma/engine`, sidecar IO (`preferences.ts`) in `@numisma/tui`. |
| [ADR-005](./ADR-005-two-plane-price-model.md) | Market data is not fund history: the two-plane price model | product | Accepted | Automatically-fetched prices enter through two decoupled planes with separate cadences: a disposable, re-fetchable price store (`data/prices/`) upserted every fetch at a configurable fetch cadence, and the sparse `PriceMarked` valuation mark emitted at a separate mark cadence through the existing validated inbox (no engine/schema change). Raw quotes are kept OUT of the append-only log as disposable data and are allowed to diverge intraday from the sparse mark. Mark-instant contract: at most one mark per instrument per mark period, `asOf` anchored to a configured trading-day timezone (not the provider's UTC candle day), self-enforced by the deterministic `pm-<id>-<asOf>` id; concrete timezone/threshold are config, the invariant is not. Rejected: folding candles into the log (bloat, slow fold, conflates valuation decision with disposable observation). ADR-001 split: `quote → PriceMarkedEvent`/symbol resolution/MXN derivation pure in `@numisma/engine`, fetch/IO/scheduling in the runtime. |
